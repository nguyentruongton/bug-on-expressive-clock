## Expressive Clock v0.1.7

This update refines the floating Timer and Stopwatch island, improves running Timer controls, and adds swipe navigation. Thanks to @pramodbeema and everyone sharing screenshots, videos and device feedback on GitHub.

### Dynamic Island

- Position the collapsed island using Android's camera-cutout bounds and status-bar dimensions, with a centred fallback below the status bar when camera alignment cannot fit safely.
- Handle overlay windows that report a zero status-bar inset, including the Pixel Fold case.
- Keep expanded readouts and action buttons in separate, centred rows, with responsive readout sizing and matching touch targets.
- Anchor expansion to the collapsed pill and reserve space inside the card for the cutout, with less unused vertical space.
- Add mechanical digit ticking, staggered movement, spring overshoot and local motion blur. Large time changes snap directly to the updated value.
- Show hours for long collapsed durations instead of thousands of minutes.
- Expand calibration ranges: horizontal offset ±200dp, vertical offset −10 to +150dp, and capsule-width adjustment −60 to +300dp, constrained by display width. Keep upward headroom to prevent the negative-padding crash without changing the spring animation.
- Correct the expanded Stopwatch reset action.

### Timer

- Fit complete button labels using measured text, including enlarged Android font settings, and centre the icon/label group.
- Remove the duplicate plus sign: the main Timer keeps a single Add icon with **10s**, while the overlay retains **+1m**. Their existing ten-second and one-minute adjustments are unchanged.
- Retain the v0.1.6 keypad separation and preset placement beneath the keypad.

### Navigation

- Swipe horizontally between Alarms, World Clock, Timer and Stopwatch on compact layouts, without wrapping at the ends.
- Use finger-following paging and spring settling, with shared bottom-navigation selection and animated navigation when tapping the bar.
- Retarget interrupted and repeated bottom-bar taps immediately, without letting an older transition overwrite the newer selection.
- Retain adjacent pages and the existing timer/stopwatch persistence and World Clock presentation state.
- Disable page swiping while alarm or city selection, settings, sheets and alarm editing are active. Keep normal vertical scrolling and child gesture handling through Compose's pager.
- Preserve the existing unfolded two-pane Alarm/World Clock layout and rotation support.

### Testing and compatibility

Checked on a base Galaxy S25 (Android 16 / One UI 8.0), including a simulated 360dp width and enlarged fonts, and Pixel 11 Pro Fold (Android 17). This does **not** physically verify the reporter's base Galaxy S26 / Android 17 / One UI 9 configuration. OPPO was unavailable for this pass. Android cutout bounds support the positioning checks; exact optical camera alignment needs a camera photograph. Manual calibration can intentionally move/enlarge the pill over system UI; automatic defaults use conservative clearance.

The APK and AAB retain the production package and signing identity. Google Play upload is handled separately by the maintainer.
