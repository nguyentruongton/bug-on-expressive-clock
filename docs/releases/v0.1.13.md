# Expressive Clock v0.1.13

A usability update shaped by your feedback. Thank you to everyone helping improve Expressive Clock!

## World Clock
- Tap the main clock to switch between analogue and digital, alongside the existing scroll gesture.
- Choose the initial clock style in Settings. Analogue remains the default.
- Retained the original compact digital size with seconds, smoother location movement and unclipped analogue glow.
- Preserved shared clock transitions and city selection on unfolded devices.

## Dynamic Island
- Automatically hides during detected immersive fullscreen experiences on Android 11 and newer, then restores without interrupting running timers or the stopwatch.
- Retains calibration and orientation visibility preferences. Older Android versions retain existing visibility.

## Widgets and Timer
- Fixed analogue widget background colour changing the hour-hand colour. Background and hand colours are now independent.
- Clarified completed Timer dismissal with a Dismiss label on the screen and notification.

## Everyday refinements
- Shortened the launcher label to Clock while retaining Expressive Clock branding inside the app.
- Alarm confirmations now use full durations, such as “1 day, 13 hours and 33 minutes”.
- Stabilised confirmation positioning when enabling alarms in quick succession.

## Verification limits
Physical checks used Galaxy S25 and Pixel 11 Pro Fold. Fullscreen checks used a controlled immersive test Activity; third-party video/game and lock-screen combinations have not all been verified. Widget rendering was compared using production RemoteViews in a test host, rather than replacing existing home-screen widgets. The final compact digital default was verified on the unfolded Fold in portrait.
