## Existing Play warnings

Owner supplied a Console screenshot for production v0.1.5/code8 showing restricted foreground services from BOOT_COMPLETED (obfuscated method d1.d), outdated androidx.fragment and edge-to-edge recommendations. Audited current BootReceiver -> AlarmScheduler/TimerScheduler/Notifications: boot only re-arms AlarmManager callbacks and notifications, with no direct startForegroundService call. AlarmReceiver starts media-playback foreground service for later exact alarm/timer callbacks. Android15+ forbids mediaPlayback service starts directly at boot (https://developer.android.com/about/versions/15/behavior-changes-15#fgs-boot-completed). A synthetic BOOT_COMPLETED broadcast on the Fold was rejected by Android as protected, so this is not a passing reboot test and the Play finding cannot be declared cleared. Do not bypass restrictions or change service types merely to suppress the warning. Recheck Play analysis after uploading code10; investigate the fresh mapped method if it persists. Fragment/edge-to-edge recommendations are existing, separate maintenance items; dependencies were not upgraded in this focused release.
