## Expressive Clock v0.1.6

This maintenance update improves Timer layouts and responsiveness across different Android devices.

- Improved Timer keypad spacing to prevent crowding on compact screens.
- Refined responsive keypad sizing with comfortable touch targets and at least 16dp separation from Start.
- Reduced unused space around the Timer digits so presets are easier to see and reach beneath the keypad.
- Improved scrolling for short windows and enlarged display/font settings.
- Preserved Timer typography, animations, foldable layouts and existing rotation support.

Tested on a Galaxy S26 Ultra running Android 16 / One UI 8.5, including a simulated 360dp width and enlarged fonts, plus a Pixel Fold on Android 17. The exact Galaxy S26 / Android 17 / One UI 9 configuration reported in issue #3 still needs confirmation. Extreme scaling and short landscape windows can require scrolling.

The APK and AAB retain the existing production package and signing identity. Google Play upload and rollout are handled separately by the maintainer.
