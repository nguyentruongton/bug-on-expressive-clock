## Expressive Clock v0.1.11

This update restores the shared clock motion on unfolded devices and refines alarm confirmations.

### Unfolded navigation
- Restore one shared analogue clock between Alarms and World Clock, moving between the right and left sides as you swipe.
- Keep the clock mounted throughout the transition, preserving its hand and readout animations.
- Preserve horizontal swipe navigation, two-pane content and the responsive bottom bar.

### Alarm confirmations
- Introduce a compact, themed capsule with a small alarm icon and a clear countdown.
- Remove the heading, icon background and drop shadow for a lighter appearance.
- Show the countdown when an alarm is enabled as well as when an enabled alarm is saved.
- Allow text to wrap at larger accessibility sizes and replace older confirmations with the latest countdown.

### Verification
Debug/release builds, unit tests, lint and Python regression checks passed. Physical Pixel 11 Pro Fold checks covered unfolded portrait shared-clock movement in both directions and navigation onward to Timer/Stopwatch and back. The earlier confirmation card was observed on-device; the final minimal variation was installed for review. A fresh automated create/enable/delete interaction cycle, landscape and other physical devices were not re-tested in this focused pass.

Thanks for the helpful feedback. Application ID and production signing identity are unchanged. Google Play upload is handled separately by the maintainer. The previously reported Play Console boot/foreground-service warning remains unresolved and is not claimed as fixed here.
