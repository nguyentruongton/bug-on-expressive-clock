## Expressive Clock v0.1.12

A small refinement to the upcoming-alarm notification:

- Shorten its action label from “Dismiss this occurrence” to “Dismiss”.
- Preserve the existing behaviour: dismissing skips only that upcoming occurrence and keeps recurring alarms enabled for their next scheduled date.

Notification timing, appearance and scheduling logic are unchanged. Application ID and production signing identity are unchanged.

Debug/release builds, unit tests, lint and Python regression checks passed. No fresh device test was performed for this text-only change; the maintainer previously confirmed the upcoming notification works physically. Google Play upload is handled separately by the maintainer. The existing Play Console foreground-service warning is not addressed by this release.
