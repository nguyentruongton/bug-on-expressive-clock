# v0.1.6 maintenance release verification

## Scope and final review

Baseline main/v0.1.5 commit: dab8f449c81c045f05d3070e847f7f6dbfa6ed08. Read AGENTS.md and reviewed all three original working-tree files plus the Samsung and Pixel Fold reports in Downloads.

The blanket portrait restriction was removed entirely: MainActivity is identical to v0.1.5, and the uncommitted PhoneOrientationTest was removed. Existing rotation support is preserved without manufacturer or hinge detection. No orientation/manifest policy change is included.

Compact Timer now budgets keypad height from its measured viewport and width-derived digit geometry, preserves 60–76dp keys, and keeps a fixed 16dp spacer between the scroll viewport and Start/running controls. The keypad retains its existing typography, elastic click/ripple/haptic handling and animation code. Setup digit cells retain their animation but have less unused vertical space. Presets stay beneath the keypad. Compact scrolling no longer adds 32dp edge gutters or fades controls, making presets or their heading/partial rows visible in the tested compact configurations. Extremely short windows still scroll. Expanded/foldable layout selection and geometry are unchanged.

TimerLayoutTest covers original sizing in roomy windows, compact space allocation, minimum touch targets on short screens and larger-font preset budgets. No alarm, storage, scheduling, widget, Installer Helper, dependency or SDK changes.
